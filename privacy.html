Kalkal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Kalkal is an app for watching AI-generated short dramas. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

Kalkal does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location. We do not sell or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your list of favorite dramas is saved only in your browser or device storage. It is not sent to our servers. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data.

Videos and images

Videos and cover images are stored with Cloudflare and delivered to your device when you watch. Like most online services, Cloudflare and the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical information such as your IP address and device type to deliver content and keep the service secure. See their privacy policies for details.

Community features

Kalkal has a public chat and lets users submit videos for review. The name you type, your chat messages, and any videos or cover images you submit are stored on our servers and may be shown to other users once approved. Do not share personal or sensitive information. We may remove any content at our discretion, and you can contact us to request removal.

Advertising and tracking

Kalkal does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Children

Kalkal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
